## 1. Divalent Cation Transport Kinetics & Enterocyte Channel Saturation

### 1.1 Apical Enterocyte Influx: TRPM6 vs TRPV6
Oral mineral absorption is rigorously governed by active transcellular and passive paracellular pathways:
1. **Active Transcellular Influx (Low-to-Moderate Luminal Loads):**
   - **Magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$):** Primary active uptake occurs via **TRPM6** (Transient Receptor Potential Melastatin 6) and its ubiquitous partner **TRPM7** on the apical brush border membrane of duodenal and jejunal enterocytes. TRPM6 is an atypical kinase-coupled channel whose activity is tightly regulated by intracellular $Mg\text{-}ATP$ and epidermal growth factor (EGF).
   - **Calcium ($Ca^{2+}$):** Primary active uptake occurs via **TRPV6** (Transient Receptor Potential Vanilloid 6), an intensely $Ca^{2+}$-selective channel regulated by $1,25(\text{OH})_2D_3$ via the Vitamin D Receptor (VDR).
2. **Competitive Antagonism Mechanism:**
   - Both $Mg^{2+}$ and $Ca^{2+}$ share common electro-chemical pathways in the intestinal lumen. When high elemental doses of Calcium ($600\text{ mg}$) and Magnesium ($400\text{ mg}$) are administered simultaneously, the massive molar surplus of $Ca^{2+}$ creates intense electrostatic competition. $Ca^{2+}$ binds with higher kinetic affinity to enterocyte junctional pores and apical channel vestibules, suppressing $Mg^{2+}$ entry through TRPM6/TRPM7 by up to **82%**.
   - **Paracellular Claudin-16 / Claudin-19 Shunt:** Paracellular electro-diffusion requires a lumen-positive transepithelial voltage gradient ($+5$ to $+10\text{ mV}$). High co-administered divalent salt concentrations collapse this gradient, precipitating severe unabsorbed mineral loss into the distal ileum and colon, triggering osmotic water shift and loose stool.

### 1.2 The DMT1 (SLC11A2) Transporter Distinction
- **Scientific Fact:** **Divalent Metal Transporter 1 (DMT1 / SLC11A2)** is a proton-coupled symporter dedicated to transition metals: Ferrous Iron ($Fe^{2+}$), Zinc ($Zn^{2+}$), Manganese ($Mn^{2+}$), and Cobalt ($Co^{2+}$).
- **Clinical Distinction:** DMT1 does *not* transport alkaline earth metals ($Mg^{2+}$, $Ca^{2+}$) under human physiological conditions. Conflating DMT1 with Magnesium transport is an amateur biochemical inaccuracy. CabinetScan AI explicitly routes mineral antagonism through **TRPM6/TRPM7, TRPV6, and Claudin-16/19**.
